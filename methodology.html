📋 Methodology

How Your Score Is Calculated

HeartGuard's risk score is a simplified, educational estimate — not a clinical diagnosis. It combines points from 16 lifestyle and health questions, loosely inspired by established risk frameworks like the Framingham Risk Score and ASCVD guidelines, adapted for accessibility.

Why We Ask Each Question

Age, BMI, blood pressure, cholesterol, smoking, diabetes status, diet, physical activity, and family history are all established contributors to cardiovascular risk in medical literature. Each question maps to a specific, evidence-based risk factor.

How Points Are Assigned

Each answer is assigned a point value (0 to 5) based on how strongly that factor is associated with cardiovascular risk in general population data. For example, daily smoking scores higher than occasional smoking; a longstanding Type 2 diabetes diagnosis scores higher than prediabetes.

Risk Categories

Total scores range from 0–51 and are grouped into four bands: Low (0–12), Mild (13–24), Moderate (25–38), and High (39–51). These thresholds are simplified cutoffs for educational clarity, not clinically validated diagnostic boundaries.

💙 This methodology is provided for transparency. HeartGuard is an educational tool and does not replace a professional cardiovascular risk assessment.
